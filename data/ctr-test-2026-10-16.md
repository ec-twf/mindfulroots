## POWER: what this report can and cannot conclude

The only page with usable volume is `saffron-extract-vs-affron` at about 245 impressions per 14 days, baseline CTR 1.224%.
To call a CTR change real rather than noise, at 80% power:

| if true CTR became | impressions needed | days at current volume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.0% | ~4,128 | ~236 |
| 2.4% | ~2,016 | ~115 |
| 3.0% | ~1,026 | ~59 |
| 5.0% | ~330 | ~19 |

So a two-week reading can only detect a very large effect. Anything subtler needs
two to eight months. Use the early reports as a **safety check and indexation read**,
and leave the CTR verdict until the impression column says it is earned.

Control arm is `quatrefolic-vs-metafolin`, deliberately not retitled. If the control
moves as much as the test pages, the cause is the URL cut, not the titles.
